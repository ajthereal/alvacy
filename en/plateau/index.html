    Why the diet that worked before doesn’t work now

    With age, metabolism and lean mass slowly decline. Eating little without enough protein can mean part of what you lose is muscle, which lowers your daily burn even more. So the same method can give different results at different ages.

    How this plateau check works

    Your answers are scored against four common patterns: energy-saving mode, blood sugar swings, thinning lean mass, and sleep and stress. The pattern with the highest score becomes your main result. It’s an educational tool to help you ask the right questions, not a medical diagnosis.

    Common questions

    Is this a diagnosis?

    No. The result is a picture of the pattern in your answers. If you have a medical condition or take regular medication, talk to your doctor first.

    Why do the full results ask for contact details?

    So your coach can reach you and help turn the result into a plan that fits. You agree first before anything is saved, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

    Do I have to join a program?

    Who should see a doctor first?

    People who are pregnant or breastfeeding, anyone under 18, and people with medical conditions like diabetes or kidney disease.
