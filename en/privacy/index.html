Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page explains what data we collect on alvacy.com, why, and what your rights are. In short: your data is only used so your coach can help you, and we never sell it to anyone.

Data we collect

  • Your Body Age Assessment answers: age range, gender, goal, and answers about eating, energy, sleep, movement, and body trend.
  • Contact details you enter yourself: name, WhatsApp number, email, city, and how you'd like to meet.
  • Visit information: where you came from (for example an Instagram link or an ad), the first page you opened, and your language choice.

The free tools (Padang, sugar, protein) run in your own browser and are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • So your coach can contact you and explain your assessment result.
  • So you and your coach can continue to a full assessment if you want to.
  • To learn which content and ads help people find us, so we can improve them.

Who can see your data

Only the coach whose link you opened and the Success Together coaching team supporting them. Data is stored in our Google Sheets, protected by our Google account.

Cookies and ad measurement

We may use the Meta Pixel to measure whether our Instagram and Facebook ads help people find this assessment. Meta may use cookies for this. You can manage ad preferences in your Meta account settings or block third-party cookies in your browser.

To count visitors, which pages are opened and where visitors come from, we use GoatCounter. GoatCounter uses no cookies and stores nothing that identifies you personally.

How long we keep it

For as long as it's relevant to helping you, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time. You can also withdraw your consent to be contacted.

How to reach us: message your coach, or WhatsApp +62 817 0241 185. We'll handle your request as soon as we can.

Not medical advice

The assessment and tools on this site are for lifestyle education and do not replace a doctor's diagnosis or advice.
