FAQ

Common questions

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. To see your full result you enter your name and contact, and your coach will reach out to walk you through it. No obligation to buy anything.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. The result shows habit signals to help you choose a first step. If you have a medical condition, keep consulting your doctor.

I’m extremely busy. Will this still work?

That’s exactly who it’s for. The focus is habits that fit a work life: eating out, meetings, business travel.

What is Omron body age?

An estimate from the Omron scale based on resting metabolism, which is shaped by your fat and muscle. Useful for tracking trends, not as an absolute number.

What is my data used for?

Only so your coach can contact you and explain your result. We don’t sell data.
